AI Resume Tips

Match the job description

Mirror the exact keywords from the job posting — most ATS engines look for literal matches.

Quantify your impact

"Improved load time by 40%" reads far stronger than "Improved website performance".

Start bullets with action verbs

Built, Designed, Automated, Led — action verbs make your contribution unmistakable.

Keep the layout simple

Avoid tables, columns of images and fancy graphics — many ATS parsers skip them entirely.

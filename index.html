About Us

Built to help you get past the first filter

Most companies use an Applicant Tracking System to scan resumes before a recruiter ever reads them. Strong candidates get rejected simply because their resume isn't structured or worded the way these systems expect.

ResumeAI closes that gap. It gives students, freshers and working professionals a clean way to build a resume, choose a professional layout, and understand how ATS-friendly their resume really is — all from the browser.
